CONVENTIONS
Each edge has weight in (0,1]. A color is feasible when its incident weight at every vertex is at most 1. chi'_w is the fewest colors. Delta_w is maximum incident weight. bp(G) is the maximum, over vertices, of the minimum number of unit bins needed for that vertex's incident edges. Parallel edges have distinct physical identities. In research flow notes, heavy edges have weight >1/2 and fluid represents divisible tiny-edge load; this relaxation is not automatically an integer coloring.

LATEST RESULTS AND GAPS

2. Audited scoped physical bridge: sparsify_localize_type_palette_bridge_candidate.md, sampled_neighborhood_finite_label_candidate.md, sparsify_localize_type_palette_independent_audit.md. Combined graph bipartite, heavy simple support a matching, all other physical weights at most sufficiently small tau: the proposed palette bound is (4/3+epsilon)bp+O_epsilon(1), uniform in graph order and minimum tiny weight. Independent agents found no gap at that scope. Temporary forest-term caps must be 12y^k, averaged caps 12 times the averaged target; average all sampled supports WITHIN each color. Determine the finite field from an a priori label-count bound before choosing palette size. This is not a theorem for arbitrary medium weights or arbitrary heavy topology.

3. Capped common-mean theorem: common_mean_capped_heavy_transfer_audit.md. Combined graph bipartite; within each connected heavy component, all endpoint-pair bundles have the same conditional mean heavy weight, but may have different distributions. For fractional heavy matching target x and fluid target y with feasible local loads and caps C>=12y, a profit-independent heavy law supports means (3x/4,3y/4). The proof uses signed mixed derivatives of independent-activation recourse, path/cycle dependent rounding, and nonnegative covariance at a selected heavy edge. It holds at the divisible one-color level even for unbounded components. Unequal means invalidate the ordinary path-rounding argument.

4. Physical transfer for bounded heavy components: bounded_heavy_component_transfer_interface.md together with the common-mean note. Both heavy and tiny targets must be scaled after sparsification. Upward bucketing needs pseudo-weight at least min(1,w/(1-3tau)); restore exact common means by category padding. Finite matching-state menus work for bounded component order. A bipartite expander prevents reducing arbitrary components to bounded ones by discarding vanishing heavy stock. The unbounded component palette remains open.

5. General graph scoped star theorem: unit_guard_capped_star_hybrid_two_thirds_audited.md and latest_star_hybrid_migration.txt. Unit-weight heavy support is a matching and fluid support is a vertex-disjoint star forest. Caps C>=12y. With probability 3/4 use independent pair states at heavy marginal 2x/3; with probability 1/4 use a common uniform threshold for pair absences at those same marginals. This law supports fluid means 2y/3. Profit-prefix estimates give a proof; neither heterogeneous heavy weights nor arbitrary fluid support follows. The migration note also gives an elementary uniform-residual sufficient regime for arbitrary fluid support.
