4 October 2026 — sharp capped two-thirds transfer on fluid paths and cycles

Status: exact scoped one-color theorem. The root's moment/flow proof was independently audited by the forest and physical-rounding agents. The forest agent then removed an unnecessary weight restriction and piecewise bound; the root checked that simplification. The abstract theorem below allows arbitrary categorical guard weights in [0,1]. It does not claim an unrestricted coloring theorem or, by itself, an integer physical palette.

MODEL
The simple support of the categorical guard edges is a matching. At pair i, guard types h have weights w_h in [0,1] and targets x_h>=0 with x_i=sum_h x_h<=1. Write a_i=sum_h w_h x_h and D_i=1-a_i. Each endpoint has allowance D_i; an unguarded vertex has D=1. Divisible fluid targets y_e>=0 have incident sum at most D_v. Each fluid edge has per-state cap C_e>=2y_e. The underlying simple support of positive fluid targets has maximum degree two; arbitrarily many parallel fluid identities/types are allowed.

THEOREM
There is one law on categorical guard matchings and feasible capped fluid flows with coordinate means exactly (2/3)x_h and (2/3)y_e. The combined graph need not be bipartite. The constant 2/3 is best possible for this class. The law and policy are explicit and independent of any profit vector.

PROOF
Put alpha=2/3. Draw one uniform U in [0,1]. Occupy pair i iff U<=alpha x_i. Conditional on occupancy, draw its type h with probability x_h/x_i, independently across pairs and independently of U. Let R_i be the common residual at its endpoints, equal to 1-W_i if occupied and 1 otherwise. A pair with x_i=0 is unoccupied. Each guard identity has marginal alpha x_h.

For x_i>0 let mu_i=a_i/x_i. Since x_i<=1, mu_i>=a_i. For distinct pairs, common occupancy thresholds give
  M_ij=E[R_i R_j]
      =1-alpha(a_i+a_j)+alpha min(a_i mu_j,a_j mu_i)
      >=1-alpha(a_i+a_j)+alpha a_i a_j
      =1-alpha+alpha D_i D_j.
For the same pair, Jensen and mu_i>=a_i give
  E[R_i^2]=1-2alpha a_i+alpha x_i E[W_i^2]
           >=1-2alpha a_i+alpha a_i^2
           =1-alpha+alpha D_i^2.
An unguarded endpoint has R=1, and E[R_j]=1-alpha+alpha D_j, so the bound still holds. Thus every fluid edge uv satisfies
  M_uv=E[R_u R_v] >= (1+2D_u D_v)/3 >=1/3.

In each sampled state put
  f_e = (2/3)y_e R_u R_v / M_uv.
This gives E[f_e]=(2/3)y_e and 0<=f_e<=2y_e<=C_e.

To check capacity at v, write s=D_v and combine parallel target values to each distinct fluid neighbor into Y_j. Every neighbor has D_j>=Y_j. Since R_j<=1,
  sum_{e incident v} f_e
    <= R_v sum_j [2Y_j/(1+2sY_j)].
The function g_s(t)=2t/(1+2st) is increasing and concave for t>=0. There are at most two neighbors, and sum_j Y_j<=s. Padding with zero if needed, Jensen gives
  sum_j g_s(Y_j) <=2g_s(s/2)=2s/(1+s^2)<=1,
where the last inequality is (1-s)^2>=0. Hence the fluid load is at most the actual residual R_v in every state. This proves all coordinate marginals and feasibility simultaneously. No separation theorem, optimization oracle, or case division is needed.

SHARPNESS OF TWO-THIRDS
Take one unit guard edge ab with target x_ab=1/2 and fluid edges ca,cb with target 1/2 each. All local rows are feasible. At a common scale alpha, the guard is present with probability alpha/2, blocking both fluid edges. Otherwise c can carry at most one unit of fluid. Desired total fluid mean is alpha, so alpha<=1-alpha/2, giving alpha<=2/3. Caps 2y=1 are inactive in this example. This is the existing triangle obstruction, now matching the scoped theorem; it is not a new unrestricted lower bound.

LOCAL BRANCH EXTENSION
For k>=2 distinct positive fluid neighbors, the same Jensen argument bounds the row multiplier by
  2s/(1+2s^2/k).
Thus the same law and policy work on any support where this is at most one at every vertex (degree-one rows also work). For k=2 this holds for all s<=1; for k=3 it holds up to s=(3-sqrt(3))/2; for arbitrary degree it holds whenever s<=1/2. High-residual branch vertices remain the unresolved case for this policy.

SHARPNESS OF CAP TWO FOR THIS LAW
Join two distinct unit-guard pairs by one fluid edge of target y=d, with both original guard presences 1-d. Under common occupancy the endpoints are free together with probability (1+2d)/3. With edge cap Kd<1, expected flow is at most Kd(1+2d)/3. Achieving (2/3)d requires K>=2/(1+2d), tending to 2 as d tends to zero. This proves only sharpness of the proportional cap premise for this fixed law.

LIMITS
Pure common occupancy can fail on diffuse stars under proportional caps. The theorem does not remove arbitrary high-residual branching. Guard supports beyond a matching and a finite integer palette with exact physical stocks are also outside this proof. A separate physical-palette audit is in progress for the degree-two fluid scope. None of these notes changes the existing unrestricted manuscript bounds.
