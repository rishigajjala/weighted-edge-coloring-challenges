4 October 2026 — explicit failure of naive proportional flow

A candidate direct flow rule was f_e=y_e times the indicator of free endpoints, divided by max(1/12,S_u,S_v), where S_v is total target on edges whose endpoints are free. This is feasible in each state and respects cap12y, but does not always give marginal >=(2/3)y under the 3/4-independent plus 1/4-comonotone unit-guard law.

Let heavy pairs be (u,u'), (v,v'), (w,w'), all unit weight. Fluid targets are y_uu'=1/5, y_uv=3/5, and y_uw=d>0. Original residual allowances are D_u=4/5+d, D_v=3/5, D_w=d. These targets form just one star, already covered by the audited optimal-recourse star theorem.

As d tends to zero, conditional on u and w free in independent mode, active target load at u is 1/5+(3/5)B, where B is v-pair availability with probability 11/15. Its reciprocal has expectation (4/15)*5+(11/15)*(5/4)=9/4. The joint availability of u,w tends to (13/15)*(1/3)=13/45, giving normalized marginal 13/20 in independent mode. In comonotone mode, when w is free every other source is free, so the normalized marginal tends to (1/3)/(4/5)=5/12. The mixture limit is (3/4)*(13/20)+(1/4)*(5/12)=71/120<2/3. By continuity, sufficiently small positive d gives a counterexample to this rule.

The failure is solely of the proposed proportional policy, not of the desired hull theorem or the revised hybrid law with optimal recourse. The example explains why a profit-independent local normalization cannot replace the star's adaptive flow proof.
