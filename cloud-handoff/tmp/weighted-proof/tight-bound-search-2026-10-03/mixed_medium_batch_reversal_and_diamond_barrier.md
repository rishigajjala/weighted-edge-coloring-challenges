# Mixed-medium batch reversal and diamond diagnostics

4 October 2026. Scratch-only negative evidence, not an unrestricted bound. The objective remains an asymptotic *fractional* local/global gap above `4/3` for bipartite support or above `3/2` for general support. Neither mechanism below supplies one.

## A count-state cycle across rank changes

Consider an alternating long path with core weights `A=.34` (rank two) and `B=.26` (rank three). On an `A->B` step the private heavy guard `.74` fits one outgoing B and rejects one incoming A. On the reverse `B->A` step the guard `.66` fits one outgoing A and one incoming B individually, but rejects a *pair* of incoming B items. Thus a reverse count-state transition exists even though the usual strictly decreasing individual-weight orientation cannot cycle.

For each vertex phase, enumerate every integer state `(incoming count,outgoing count,guard count)` of load at most one. A local stationary law requires only equality of adjacent **mean** core counts. A globally consistent stationary color law requires equality of the full adjacent core-count histograms; this is a circulation on the finite count-state transfer graph. The script `alternating_rank_batch_guard_stationary.py` constructs the two LPs. The companion `alternating_rank_batch_guard_polar.py` enumerates 59 elementary transfer cycles, 38 distinct global mean-profit patterns (12 nondominated), and 36 vertices of the nonnegative global polar. The largest computed local value at a polar vertex is `1.25` (floating LP/geometry tolerance), with profit direction `(A,B,guard_reverse,guard_descend)=(1/2,0,1/2,1/2)`. The rank-three B bundle has zero profit at the optimizer. This is a targeted diagnostic, not a rational certificate for an exact template theorem.

To make a true three-item guarded bin, use alternating core weights `.334` (rank two) and `.251` (rank three), reverse *medium* guard `.49`, and descending heavy guard `.749`. The reverse guard can coexist with one `.334` or two `.251` items, but not with one of each. It may appear twice in a color, and the enumeration includes both copies. `alternating_medium_guard_reverse_polar.py` enumerates 80 elementary transfer cycles, 48 distinct patterns (11 nondominated), and 27 nonnegative polar vertices. Its largest computed ratio is `7/6`, at profit `(0,1/3,1/3,1/3)`, which drops the rank-two core. Again this is floating diagnostic evidence only. The hoped-for batch-level directed cycle does not amplify the guarded-path `4/3` obstruction in these two concrete templates.

## Why a single mixed-size guarded diamond dilutes

A physical bipartite diamond illustrates the local synergy but not amplification. Put vertices `v,a,u,b` on the cycle `v-a-u-b-v`, plus a private leaf at `v`. Give `v-a` a bundle A of `2d` edges of weight `.251`, `v-b` a bundle B of `2d` edges of weight `.201`, and the private leaf edge bundle H of `2d` edges of weight `.54`. Put `d` unit guard edges on each of `a-u` and `b-u`. For `d` divisible by three, local bin count is exactly `2d`: at `v`, each H pairs with one A and one B (`.992` total); at `u`, the two unit guard bundles need `2d` bins; the other vertices need fewer.

In a hypothetical `2d`-coloring, the unit guards partition the palette into d colors of each guard type, and H occupies every color. A cannot use its own guard colors; in the other d colors, H+.251 permits at most one A per color, short of the `2d` A edges. More generally, with `M=2d+T`, at most `d+T` colors avoid A's guard, at most T colors lack H, and an H-free color contains at most three A edges. Thus A capacity is at most `d+3T`, forcing `T>=d/3`. The same inequality holds after weighting colors fractionally, so this is a physical fractional count obstruction, but only ratio `7/6`, below the known `4/3`.

The bound is attained for `T=d/3`: use `d` colors with the `a-u` guard, H and two B edges; `d` colors with the `b-u` guard, H and one A edge; and `d/3` extra colors with three A edges. Thus the mixed H+A+B bin alone cannot improve the known bound. Replicating this isolated diamond does not amplify its ratio; a successful construction would need a network that makes the per-diamond missing colors accumulate rather than reuse the same extra palette.
