FINITE PHYSICAL CALENDAR FOR MATCHING-HEAVY / STAR-FOREST-TINY GRAPHS
Independent audit, 5 October 2026

STATUS AND SCOPE

The bounded-label law in oct5_bounded_labels_mixed_star_hybrid_audit.txt
and the pre-deletion conditional-flow theorem in
oct5_mixed_star_forest_fixed_hybrid_beta_eps_transfer.txt together admit
an exact finite physical palette. The argument below checks the
denominators, heavy stock, dummy types, tiny caps, and 4*tau reserve.
It assumes physical heavy weights STRICTLY greater than 1/2, heavy
simple support a matching, tiny edge weights at most tau, and tiny
simple support a vertex-disjoint star forest. It does not handle
arbitrary medium weights or arbitrary tiny branching.

Let N=max(H,Delta_w), where H is the maximum physical heavy-edge count
at a vertex and Delta_w is the maximum weighted degree. Both are at
most bp(G): H<=bp uses physical heavy weights >1/2. For a fixed
accuracy eta>0, choose constants

  alpha=2/3, beta=alpha-epsilon0, kappa>=64/epsilon0,
  0<tau<1/4, sigma>0, Q integer,

with tau, sigma, and B/Q sufficiently small in terms of eta; B is
the number of upward heavy-weight buckets, at most ceil(1/sigma)+1.
Take Q large enough that B/(alpha Q)<1 and the palette period below
is at least kappa/beta. These are fixed constants independent of the
number of vertices, edges, and the smallest tiny weight.

1. BOUNDED LABELS AND PERIOD

Use the source-label construction of the cited bounded-label audit,
with split parameter q_split=ceil(2*kappa). It produces at most
L=(4(floor(16*kappa)+1)+1)*q_split labels. The center source is marked
and unique at its star. Every prefix-strong leaf group has a unique
label. Each label's unmarked weak groups at a star have aggregate
whole-star mass at most 3/(4*kappa). Unguarded weak leaves form one
deterministic block; an unguarded strong leaf alone supplies the
needed rank. Thus the all-strong and weak-product arguments survive
seed sharing within labels. A heavy source meets at most two stars,
which is the essential sparsity for the label construction.

Use one Q-point uniform coordinate for occupancy and one for type
at each label, plus one Q-point common occupancy coordinate. Give
independent mode weight 3/4 and common mode weight 1/4. Enumerating
all tuples with three copies of independent mode and one of common
mode gives a period

  P = 4 Q^(2L+1).

Redundant coordinates are harmless. Each coordinate grid is chosen
as Q equally weighted points representing threshold intervals of
length 1/Q. Source occupancy and categorical type thresholds below
are multiples of 1/Q, so every source/type has its exact prescribed
marginal in this finite period. Correlations only occur among sources
sharing a label and are already covered by the bounded-label audit.

2. PALETTE SIZE AND ROUNDED INPUT

Choose M as the smallest multiple of P satisfying

  M >= max{ kappa/beta,
    (1+4*tau+sigma)*N /
      [beta*(1-4*tau)*(1-B/(alpha*Q))] }.

Then M is chosen BEFORE applying the label construction; M=O_eta(N)+
O_eta(1), M>=N, M is divisible by Q^2, and beta*M>=kappa.

For each physical heavy edge of weight w, use its upward surrogate

  w' = min(1, sigma*ceil((w+4*tau)/sigma)).

There are at most B surrogate types per matched heavy pair. If w'<1,
then w'>=w+4*tau; if w'=1, its surrogate occupied residual is zero.
Also w'<=w+4*tau+sigma. A heavy pair has d physical identities,
of which d_j have surrogate type j. Because d<=H<=N<=M, define

  p=floor(Q*d/M)/Q,
  r_j=floor(Q*d_j/d)/Q       (d>0),
  r_0=1-sum_j r_j.

The type 0 is a dummy of surrogate weight one; set p=0 when d=0.
These quantities lie on the Q-grid. Give the abstract source target
x_pair=p/alpha and conditional type probabilities r_0,r_1,... .
Its pre-deletion heavy-law marginal for type j is p*r_j. Define
fluid target y_e=w_e/[beta*M*(1-4*tau)] and per-state cap
C_e=w_e/(1-4*tau). Then C_e/y_e=beta*M>=kappa. The latter equality
also ensures that after scaling the eventual per-color fluid amount
by 1-4*tau it never exceeds physical edge weight w_e.

At each vertex the abstract target row is at most one. Indeed the
real-type heavy target load is bounded by
  sum_j (p/alpha) r_j w'_j
    <= [W_heavy(v)+(4*tau+sigma)*d(v)]/(alpha*M).
The dummy target load is at most p*r_0/alpha<=B/(alpha*Q), because
r_0<B/Q and p<=1. The tiny target load is
W_tiny(v)/[beta*M*(1-4*tau)]. Since alpha>=beta,
W_heavy+W_tiny<=N, and d(v)<=N, their sum is at most

  (1+4*tau+sigma)*N/[beta*M*(1-4*tau)]
     + B/(alpha*Q) <=1.

Also x_pair=p/alpha<=1, since M>=N/alpha. The star-forest abstract
theorem therefore applies with the bounded-label heavy law.

3. EXACT BULK HEAVY STOCK

Enumerate the P seed states and repeat the period M/P times. In
EITHER hybrid mode, source i has occupancy probability p and
conditional real type j probability r_j, so the calendar has exactly

  b_j=M*p*r_j

type-j occupied slots at that physical pair. Because M is divisible
by Q^2, b_j is an integer. Since p<=d/M and r_j<=d_j/d,
b_j<=d_j. Assign distinct physical type-j edges to these slots;
delete every dummy slot. Deletion only increases residual capacity.

The unassigned heavy stock at the pair is

  d - sum_j b_j = (d-M*p)+M*p*r_0
                  < M/Q + d*B/Q
                  <= (B+1)*M/Q.

All unassigned heavy identities can be colored in fewer than
(B+1)*M/Q+1 extra colors, because the heavy simple support is a
matching: use the same leftover color across all different pairs.
No per-identity approximation or denominator depending on graph size
is needed. The physical calendar must use the PRE-DELETION alpha
heavy law; the final beta/alpha deletion in the abstract theorem is
only for its equal heavy/fluid marginal statement and is omitted here.

4. EXACT TINY STOCK AND ROW RESERVE

The proof of the abstract theorem supplies a conditional fluid flow
for EACH finite seed state whose mean is exactly beta*y_e for every
tiny identity. This is a finite LP: the state law is the uniform
period above, the per-state star flow constraints are linear, and
the bounded-label support-function proof guarantees feasibility.
Choose one such flow in each state and scale every flow by 1-4*tau.
Across M repeated colors, the total scaled flow of edge e is

  M*(1-4*tau)*beta*y_e=w_e.

If F_ec denotes the scaled flow in color c, set z_ec=F_ec/w_e.
Then sum_c z_ec=1 and 0<=z_ec<=1, the latter following from
C_e=w_e/(1-4*tau). At a vertex-color row, the fractional physical
load is at most 1-4*tau when no surrogate heavy is present. If a
surrogate heavy of weight w'<1 is present, the scaled fluid load is
at most (1-4*tau)*(1-w')<=1-w'; the actual physical heavy weighs
w<=w'-4*tau, so the row has at least 4*tau slack. If w'=1, the
surrogate row has zero flow, hence every z_ec touching it is fixed
zero; after replacing dummy slots by empty slots, physical slack
can only increase. Thus every row that has a positive fractional
tiny assignment has at least 4*tau slack against its ACTUAL core.

Apply the two-row assignment iterative-rounding lemma from
oct5_arbitrary_heavy_mixed_tiny_physical_palette_audit.txt to the
variables z_ec: each edge identity has equality sum_c z_ec=1 and
each variable touches two vertex-color capacity rows. Permanently
fix zero variables. A basic-solution count finds an active row with
at most four fractional variables; dropping it can add at most
4*tau weighted load. Repeating yields one physical color for every
tiny identity without exceeding any actual row capacity. Zero-flow
surrogate weight-one rows stay forbidden, so no tiny edge leaks into
one of them. Parallel tiny identities and unbounded tiny degree cause
no change in the counting argument.

5. CONSEQUENCE AND LIMITS

The total number of colors is at most

  M + ceil((B+1)*M/Q)
   <= [1+O(B/Q)]*(1+4*tau+sigma)*N /
       [beta*(1-4*tau)*(1-B/(alpha Q))]
       + O_epsilon(1).

Choosing epsilon0,tau,sigma,B/Q small enough gives, for every fixed
eta>0,

  chi'_w(G) <= (3/2+eta)*bp(G) + O_eta(1)

for this restricted matching-heavy/star-forest-tiny physical class.
The implicit constant contains P and is extremely large. The proof
is finite and constructive for rational weights, but its dependence
on eta is not practical. It makes no claim for a physical weight
exactly 1/2 in the heavy class: H<=bp can fail there, and the abstract
source constraint x_pair<=1 could then fail. Nor does it resolve
nonmatching heavy support or branching fluid support, the barriers
for unrestricted weighted multigraphs.

AUDIT CAUTIONS

* Distinguish q_split for source sublabels from Q for the seed grid.
* Build labels after M and the y_e have been chosen, but use the
  a priori bound L in defining P before M; this is not circular.
* Use pre-deletion alpha heavy slots and beta fluid means.
* The dummy type must be included in the abstract local weighted
  row and assigned surrogate weight one, then physically deleted.
* Require beta*M>=kappa for the individual tiny cap check.
* Round each physical heavy weight upward by 4*tau before solving
  fluid recourse; merely subtracting 4*tau from each residual could
  destroy an unbounded number of tiny leaf capacities.
