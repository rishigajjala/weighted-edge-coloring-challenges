FIXED-k PHYSICAL PALETTE COROLLARY
The last section of degree_two_fluid_matching_heavy_physical_palette_audit.md extends its scoped physical construction from simple tiny-support degree two to any fixed degree k>=2. With matching simple support for guards, strict guard weights >1/2, all other physical weights <=tau(epsilon,k), and N bounding each weighted degree and guard-pair count, it gives chi'_w <=(2-1/k+epsilon)N+O_{epsilon,k}(1); for the strict heavy case the indicated N is <=bp(G). Put alpha_k=k/(2k-1). The common-prefix heavy law has E[R_uR_v]>=1-alpha_k+alpha_k D_uD_v; f_e=alpha_k y_e R_uR_v/E[R_uR_v] has exact mean alpha_k y_e and cap <=k/(k-1)y_e. Its row bound follows by Jensen over at most k tiny bundles: sum g_d(Y)<=k g_d(d/k)<=1, with the last inequality equivalent to (1-d)(k-1-d)>=0. A (2k+1)-label pair-interaction coloring and fixed q^2 field blocks supply the physical pairwise moments, ghost heavy slots, exact heavy stock, and source-preserving tiny rounding. This is 3/2 at k=2, 5/3 at k=3, and tends to 2; it says nothing about unbounded simple tiny degree or nonmatching heavy support.

DISTINCT-CENTER CAPPED BRIDGE, NOW INDEPENDENTLY AUDITED
oct4_distinct_center_bridge_hybrid_capped.md and oct4_distinct_center_bridge_hybrid_independent_audit.md prove a two-thirds support-function transfer for a fixed five-bundle divisible-fluid graph: two two-spoke stars with distinct centers joined by one bridge, and four disjoint unit-heavy matching-pair sources. Each conditional fluid cap is at least 12 times its target. The fixed heavy law is 3/4 independent scaled pair-free states plus 1/4 comonotone scaled states. For arbitrary local allowances, parallel fluid identities, and nonnegative fluid profits, its conditional capped recourse has the required two-thirds support value; hence the target fluid marginals transfer by separation. The independent audit found no material gap at precisely this scope.

The repaired proof uses a statewise lower model, not separate capped-star optima. Its six profit rays require direct floor charges. The OR-event ray has sharp uncapped margin 7/96; the genuinely four-source (1,1,1) ray has sharp margin 1/8. These pay the appropriate cap floors. The earlier shortcut of summing actual optimized capped stars was invalid because each star may mix spoke and bridge flow in ways the joint lower model cannot simultaneously realize. The theorem gives neither arbitrary-fluid-graph transfer, heterogeneous heavy types, nor a finite physical palette.

ARBITRARY-CORE SURPLUS AND ITS WEIGHTED LIMIT
oct4_branch_surplus_uncapped_checkpoint.md proves a scoped equal-profit support inequality for arbitrary core topology (including cycles, parallels, unbounded degree). Each core center is unguarded and has a nonempty positive-target private spoke star; stars are vertex-disjoint; leaves may be paired by unit-heavy guards; all fluid spokes are uncapped and can deliver a full unit when available. The fixed hybrid law gives a single-star expected unit-profit recourse H(s)>=(1+s)/3 for spoke mass s. Summing over centers, the surplus (1-s)/3 pays all equal-profit core target because core incidence r obeys s+r<=1 and each core edge is charged at two centers. One may ignore every core edge in the selected statewise pattern and still reach two-thirds of total target profit. The theorem is a support-objective barrier to an all-edge-count lower construction in this architecture, not coordinatewise core coverage.

The same note proves one weighted-profit cone: if every positive spoke at v has profit at least a_v>0 and each core edge uv has q_uv<=(a_u+a_v)/2, the spoke-only policy pays two-thirds of the spoke-plus-core target. oct4_guarded_paired_spoke_arbitrary_core_count_barrier.md gives a related sharper local charge for exactly two private leaves forming one heavy pair per center: their joint free probability R(a,b)>=(b+s)/3; arbitrary unit-profit core follows, and weighted core is allowed when q_uv<=(M_u+M_v)/2 with M_v the largest spoke profit. These claims require private, nonreused spokes and uncapped full-unit recourse. They do not cover high-price core edges, cap-12 spokes, an empty positive spoke prefix, or general target hull inclusion.

EXACT DUAL-ORIENTATION OBSTRUCTION
The weighted surplus cone suggests orienting every positive spoke from a lower center dual price to a higher leaf dual price. oct4_branch_surplus_uncapped_checkpoint.md disproves this as a universal decomposition, even for an uncapped q-optimal target. On fluid path 1-2-3-4 take profits (3,6,7), y_e=1/5, allowances (1/5,2/5,2/5,1/5), and unit-heavy pair sources (1,4),(2,3). The dual pi=(1,2,4,3) certifies q-optimality with value 16/5. Every tight dual has pi=(t,3-t,3+t,4-t). The only disjoint private-star/core decomposition uses centers 2,3, spokes 1-2 and 3-4, and core 2-3. Making both spokes price-favorable forces t>=3/2 and t<=1/2, impossible. This obstructs that particular dual-oriented proof, not the desired two-thirds transfer: a separate degree-two theorem handles the path. In capped instances, a q-optimal replacement can additionally violate the original edge caps.

PRIMARY-LITERATURE CHECK
oct4_demand_matching_local_hull_literature_audit.md identifies our one-color relaxation as intersection of exact one-vertex knapsack hulls P=intersection K_v, with an edge consuming the same weight at both endpoints and every vertex capacity one. Goemans-Pan (arXiv:2609.17932) studies this demand-matching Knapsack-LP with arbitrary vertex capacities; its simple-graph upper gaps 2 bipartite and 3/2+sqrt(2) general are weaker than our targets. Its larger displayed lower gaps concern the weaker Natural-LP, and its Appendix A is sharpness of an independent-proposal contention-resolution scheme, not an exact-local-hull lower witness. Its multigraph approximation uses an auxiliary multiple-choice LP, so it does not directly settle the original physical-edge multigraph hull. Path-flow knapsack-intersection, arbitrary two-column packing, and graph-balancing lower examples likewise fail the two-endpoint exact-local-hull model for distinct reasons recorded in the audit. An unequal-capacity or single-copy witness cannot be imported as a uniform-capacity, repeatable-bundle coloring lower bound without rechecking local configurations and bp.

NEXT DIRECTIONS AND SHORTCUTS TO AVOID
The substantive open transfer is capped two-thirds hybrid recourse on arbitrary branching support, especially high-price core edges and empty spoke prefixes; a quantitative surplus invariant or grouped paired-source elimination may work. Beyond that, heterogeneous guard weights, unbounded tiny degree, nonmatching heavy support, and a graph-order-uniform physical palette remain separate gaps. For a new lower bound, seek a repeatable mixed-medium gadget whose fractional local point and asymptotic bp ratio survive replication; equal-profit guarded-core branching and simple factor parity did not do so. Do not infer a full hull theorem from a single profit cone, a physical palette from a divisible support inequality, or a >4/3 or >3/2 coloring lower bound from a fixed-law or independent-sampling failure.
