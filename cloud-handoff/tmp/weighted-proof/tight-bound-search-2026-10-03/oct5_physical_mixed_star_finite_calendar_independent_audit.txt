Independent audit of the finite physical calendar for mixed star forests
5 October 2026. Scratch only. No manuscript edit.

Scope checked: physical heavy weights strictly above 1/2, heavy simple
support a matching, tiny weights at most tau, tiny support a vertex-
disjoint star forest (parallel tiny identities allowed). Let N be the
maximum of weighted degree and heavy edge-count degree; N<=bp.

I read oct5_mixed_star_forest_fixed_hybrid_beta_eps_transfer.txt,
oct5_bounded_labels_mixed_star_hybrid_audit.txt,
physical_tiny_cap_rounding_route.md, and
oct5_physical_heavy_tiny_product_rounding_audit.txt, and checked the
proposed period/calendar formulas communicated by the rounding agent.
The checks below are conditional on the abstract full-prefix transfer
and bounded-label lemma as stated in those notes. I found no stock or
rounding contradiction in the calendar described here.

Parameters and exact finite law. Fix target eta>0. Choose
0<epsilon<=1/6, beta=2/3-epsilon, kappa>=64/epsilon,
s=1-4tau>0, bucket width sigma>0, J=O(1/sigma) bucket weights, and
an integer grid Q with J/(alpha Q)<1, alpha=2/3. The number L of
heavy-source labels is O(kappa^2), independent of graph order.
Take period P=4Q^(2L+1) and choose bulk color count M a multiple of
P. All random choices can then be represented by exact uniform seed
enumeration: mode 3/4 independent and 1/4 common, U/V grid variables
for each label, and one common U grid variable. A seed state appears
exactly M/P times. Both occupancy probabilities and categorical bucket
probabilities are multiples of 1/Q, so every expected slot count times
M is an integer. This is the PRE-deletion alpha-heavy law; using the
post-deletion beta-heavy law would undercount heavy slots.

Heavy stock. At a heavy pair let d_j physical edges lie in bucket j,
d=sum_j d_j. For d>0 set

  p=floor(Qd/M)/Q,
  r_j=floor(Qd_j/d)/Q,
  r_0=1-sum_j r_j.

For d=0 use p=0. Feed the abstract law bucket targets
x_j=p r_j/alpha and a dummy unit-weight target x_0=p r_0/alpha.
Every bulk color selects bucket j at the pair in exactly M p r_j
slots, and M p r_j<=d_j. Assign distinct physical edges of that
bucket injectively to those slots. The actual weight never exceeds
the upward surrogate bucket weight. Delete dummy selections; this
only increases residual capacity. The total number of unassigned
physical heavy identities at the pair is

  d-Mp sum_j r_j=(d-Mp)+Mp r_0 <(J+1)M/Q.

Heavy support is a matching, so all leftover identities fit in fewer
than (J+1)M/Q additional heavy-only colors, simultaneously across
all pairs. The bound is independent of the number of pairs.

Bucket inflation and local input. Bucket each physical heavy weight
w upward to w'=min(1,ceil((w+4tau)/sigma)sigma). Thus w'>=w+4tau
unless w'=1, and always w'<=w+4tau+sigma. For each tiny physical
identity e put y_e=w_e/(beta M s) and use the minimal fluid cap
C_e=kappa y_e. The pre-deletion law has heavy bucket marginal
alpha x_j=p r_j; its conditional fluid policy has exact mean
beta y_e. Including the dummy load, the pseudo-heavy target row at
a pair is at most

  [L_v+(4tau+sigma+J/Q)d]/(alpha M),

where L_v is actual heavy weighted load and d its heavy count. The
tiny target row is T_v/(beta M s). Since L_v+T_v<=N and d<=N,
and beta s<alpha, a sufficient local condition is

  M/N >= 1/(beta s)+(4tau+sigma+J/Q)/alpha.       (A)

The proposed stronger calendar choice

  M/N >= (1+4tau+sigma)/[beta s(1-J/(alpha Q))]

implies (A): after multiplying by beta s, its left coefficient is
(1+a)/(1-b)>=1+a+b, where a=4tau+sigma and b=J/(alpha Q), while
the right coefficient of (A) is 1+(beta s/alpha)a+beta s b.
Also p/alpha<=d/(alpha M)<=1 when M>=N/alpha, so the categorical
pair target is admissible. The dummy term is essential in this check;
discarding it before the local-row test would be invalid.

Tiny stock and row reserve. For each seed state solve the star-wise
conditional capped-flow LP with exact means beta y_e; repeat the
seed table M/P times. Multiply every fluid load by s. Each physical
tiny identity then has total fractional stock

  M*s*beta*y_e=w_e

exactly, with per-color load at most s*kappa*y_e
=kappa*w_e/(beta M). For M>=kappa/beta, its per-color inclusion
fraction is at most one. This lower bound on M can be included as an
eta-dependent additive palette floor. When a bucket-heavy edge is
selected and w'<1, pseudo residual 1-w' is at least 4tau below the
actual residual 1-w; multiplying fluid by s only increases that
slack. If w'=1, pseudo residual is zero and no tiny flow touches that
pair in that color. When the pair is free, scaling by s leaves 4tau
slack. Dummy deletion and replacing a bucket by a lighter physical
identity can only help. Thus every row used by tiny flow has at
least 4tau reserve below its actual residual.

The general two-endpoint source-preserving LP rounding in
oct5_physical_heavy_tiny_product_rounding_audit.txt applies to the
finite table: fix zeros permanently, drop a tight vertex-color row
with at most four fractional edge-color variables, and later lose at
most 4tau there. It uses no bipartiteness or bound on tiny parallel
multiplicity. Rows with pseudo residual zero have no positive option.
Consequently each tiny physical edge is assigned exactly one bulk
color and every actual vertex-color capacity remains at most one.

Parameter order and additive term. Choose epsilon, sigma and then Q
large enough (with J=O(1/sigma)) that

  [(1+4tau+sigma)/(beta s(1-J/(alpha Q)))]
      *[1+(J+1)/Q] < 3/2+eta

for all sufficiently small tau. This is possible because the left
side tends to 1/beta as sigma,tau,J/Q tend to zero, and beta can be
chosen arbitrarily close to 2/3. Round M upward to a multiple of
P=4Q^(2L+1); this adds only O_eta(1) colors. Ensure also
M>=kappa/beta and M>=N/alpha by another O_eta(1) floor. Then bulk
plus leftover-heavy colors are at most (3/2+eta)N+O_eta(1), hence
(3/2+eta)bp+O_eta(1). The actual weighted graph may have arbitrarily
many heavy pairs or arbitrarily small positive tiny weights; neither
enters L,P or the additive term.

Audit caveat. The proof must explicitly use the finite-seed version
of the bounded-label full-prefix inequality, yielding one conditional
fluid LP with exact means beta y under the PRE-deletion heavy law.
The abstract theorem's final heavy thinning is omitted in this
physical construction. If the finite law only guaranteed support
after thinning, or if label-sharing failed a star prefix, the stock
calculation above would not close. The bounded-label audit states the
needed pre-deletion support. Also, proof-only virtual guards at
unguarded leaves must not be counted as physical sources or calendar
labels; the label audit handles them by a deterministic weak block
and direct strong-leaf argument.
