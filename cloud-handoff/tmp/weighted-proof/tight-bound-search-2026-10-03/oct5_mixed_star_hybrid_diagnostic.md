# Mixed-weight star hybrid: numerical status and a failed chord reduction

5 October 2026. This is evidence and proof-method diagnostics, not a general mixed-heavy star theorem. The fixed law samples categorical heavy pairs independently in a `3/4` mode and by one common occupancy threshold in a `1/4` mode, always with heavy identity marginals `2x_h/3`. Its exact finite-state star evaluator is `oct5_mixed_star_hybrid_probe.py`.

In 10,000 sampled stars with one guarded center and up to four leaf-pair groups (one or two fluid leaves per group), one or two categorical types per pair, weights among `.500001,.6,.75,1`, and individual caps `12y`, no unit-profit recourse violation was found. The smallest observed ratio `E recourse / [(2/3)Σy]` was about `1.02551`. Differential-evolution runs on one- and two-group single-type families also found no violation; a known unit-guard boundary has exact ratio one. These computations are not a certificate for arbitrary profits, supports, or parameters.

A tempting analytic shortcut is false even with all heavy weights at least `1/2`: replace each real residual `R` by a conditionally Bernoulli bit of mean `R`, then apply the audited unit-guard star theorem to the induced bit law. Take a two-leaf star with center guard weight `1/2`, original presence `1/5` (`D_c=9/10`), and a single heavy pair across both leaves of weight `11/20`, original presence one (`D_leaf=9/20`). Put target `y=9/20` on each spoke, so total target is `S=9/10`; caps `12y` are inactive at one-color capacity one. In the independent mode the binary-chord center/leaf free intersection is `133/225`; in the common-occupancy mode it is `181/300`. Their hybrid average is `713/1200`, **below** the required `2S/3=3/5=720/1200`. Thus the binary-reduced lower estimate loses `7/1200`.

The actual real-residual star has ample recourse: the independent-mode value is `197/225`, the common-mode value is `22/25`, and their hybrid average is `263/300>3/5`. A present leaf heavy edge still leaves residual `9/20` at both leaves, supplying `9/10` total fluid capacity; replacing it by a binary blocker discards precisely the slack that repairs the apparent deficit. Any mixed-weight star proof must keep a quantitative account of occupied-state residuals, not just the mean binary activation probabilities.

Separate exact positive scopes now available are `oct5_mixed_guard_unguarded_center_star_transfer.md` (unguarded centers, arbitrary mixed leaf guards and degree, independent law) and the candidate `oct5_unit_center_mixed_leaf_star_conditional_transfer.md` (unit center guard, arbitrary mixed leaf guards, one star, centered conditional law). The remaining high-load branch case has a nonunit center guard. The load-half product-flow theorem in `oct5_mixed_center_branch_dichotomy.md` resolves all vertices of target fluid load at most `1/2` regardless of graph topology.
