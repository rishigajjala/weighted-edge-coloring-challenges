FIXED-HYBRID DIVISIBLE-FLUID THEOREM

The proof and separate audit are in
oct5_mixed_star_forest_fixed_hybrid_beta_eps_transfer.txt and
oct5_mixed_star_forest_fixed_hybrid_independent_audit.txt. Heavy sources
are categorical parallel-edge pairs whose simple support is a matching;
their possible weights lie in [1/2,1]. Fluid identities may be parallel
and have arbitrary positive targets on a vertex-disjoint star forest.
Every local target row obeys heavy weighted load plus fluid target load
at most one. Every fluid identity has a per-state cap at least K times
its target. For 0<epsilon<=1/6, K>=64/epsilon, and beta=2/3-epsilon,
there is one global law of feasible one-color heavy choices and divisible
fluid flows with exact beta-scaled coordinate marginals.

The law first samples a global mode: probability 3/4 independent heavy
pair occupancy and probability 1/4 occupancy from one common uniform
variable. In both modes a pair's selected heavy type has its prescribed
categorical distribution. Before final heavy thinning, each heavy type
has marginal alpha x with alpha=2/3, while a conditional fluid policy
has exact mean beta y. Deleting selected heavies with probability
1-beta/alpha gives the theorem's equal beta marginals. The law depends
on local targets, not on a selected fluid profit vector.

The proof controls every profit prefix. A capped leaf group with prefix
mass at least 1/K is strong. Endpoint concavity reduces its adverse
occupied heavy residual to 0 or 1/2; the all-strong algebra handles any
number of unequal strong groups under the same hybrid law. Weak groups
contribute an independent-mode product bound. Large weak mass is paid by
that product bound plus the common all-free event; small weak mass can
be discarded with an epsilon loss, and small total prefixes have direct
rank bounds. A leaf that is the center's own heavy partner is handled
separately. Greedy star recourse maximizes every prefix rank in profit
order, so layer-cake gives all nonnegative profit support inequalities.
Vertex-disjoint stars add statewise even when a heavy pair joins two
different stars. Separation yields exact simultaneous fluid means.
The independent audit checked the endpoint reduction, all-strong
algebra, weak-group cases, own-partner case, and global assembly and
reported no gap at this stated abstract scope.

This replaces the phase-10 limitation: the earlier isolated-star
conditional laws failed to glue unchanged across unequal stars joined
by shared heavy pairs. The fixed 3/4-independent, 1/4-common hybrid is
one common law for all unequal stars in the matching-heavy star-forest
setting. The old nested-stratum example remains a counterexample to
that particular older law, not to the new hybrid or to existence.

BOUNDED SOURCE LABELS

oct5_bounded_labels_mixed_star_hybrid_audit.txt proves that this law
needs only L=O(K^2) independent source labels, regardless of graph
size. A heavy pair touches at most two vertex-disjoint stars. At each
star mark its center source and every leaf source of whole-star mass
at least delta=1/(16K). At most D=floor(16K)+1 sources are marked.
The conflict graph joins sources sharing a star when at least one is
marked there. Every induced subgraph has at most 2D edges per source,
so a (4D+1)-coloring makes each marked source unique at its star.
Split each base color into ceil(2K) sublabels by a two-row assignment
LP. At an unmarked star each final label then has whole-star leaf mass
at most 3/(4K); at a marked star its base color occurs only once.

All prefix-strong leaf groups therefore have distinct labels from
each other and the center, preserving the independence needed by the
all-strong proof. Weak groups sharing a label have aggregate lower-model
rank at most 3/4 and mean at least K/3 times their aggregate prefix
mass. Independent label blocks retain the weak product bound. In
common mode the event that every heavy pair is absent is unchanged.
Unguarded strong leaves give a direct rank bound; unguarded weak leaves
form a deterministic block, so they do not consume labels or vanish
from the weak estimate. This label result is a coupling lemma, not by
itself a physical coloring or a finite denominator argument.

FINITE PHYSICAL CALENDAR AND TWO AUDITS

The complete construction is in
oct5_finite_calendar_mixed_star_forest_physical_transfer_audit.txt;
oct5_physical_mixed_star_finite_calendar_independent_audit.txt checks
the same stock and rounding bridge independently. Both rely on the
abstract full-prefix and label lemmas above, and found no calendar
contradiction at the stated scope. Let the physical heavy weights be
strictly greater than 1/2, with matching simple support. Let all other
physical weights be at most tau, with vertex-disjoint star-forest
simple support; tiny parallels and unbounded tiny degree are allowed.
Put N=max{maximum heavy count at a vertex, maximum weighted degree}.
Then N<=bp. The strict >1/2 condition matters: exact half-weight
parallel stock can have heavy count greater than bp.

Fix eta>0. Choose beta=2/3-epsilon, K>=64/epsilon, sufficiently small
tau and heavy bucket width sigma, and a large finite seed grid Q.
Choose a priori label bound L=O(K^2), period P=4Q^(2L+1), and bulk
palette size M divisible by P. The 4 encodes the hybrid 3/4 versus
1/4 mode weights. Every physical heavy weight w is rounded upward to
the surrogate min{1, sigma ceil((w+4tau)/sigma)}. At a heavy pair with
d physical items and bucket counts d_j, set

  p=floor(Qd/M)/Q,  r_j=floor(Qd_j/d)/Q,

and give the missing conditional probability to a dummy unit-weight
type. The dummy must be included in the abstract local weighted row.
Taking M at least a fixed constant times N plus a fixed additive
period ensures those rows are at most one, p/(2/3)<=1, and the fluid
cap multiplier is at least K. The seed table is the PRE-deletion heavy
law, with exact beta y fluid means. Using the post-thinning law here
would undercount physical heavy slots.

Each real bucket j occupies exactly M p r_j slots. M is divisible by
Q^2, so this is an integer, and p<=d/M and r_j<=d_j/d make it at
most d_j. Assign distinct physical heavy items to those slots and
delete dummy slots. Fewer than (B+1)M/Q heavy items remain per pair,
where B=O(1/sigma) is the number of buckets. Because heavy support
is a matching, all leftovers fit into that many extra shared colors,
independent of the number of pairs.

For each physical tiny edge e, use abstract fluid target
y_e=w_e/[beta M(1-4tau)] and cap C_e=w_e/(1-4tau); M is chosen so
beta M>=K. Solve the finite conditional-flow LP on the seed states,
repeat the period, and scale each flow by 1-4tau. Its total flow
across M bulk colors is exactly w_e, and each color's fractional
inclusion is at most one. A free surrogate row has 4tau slack after
scaling; an occupied surrogate of weight below one is at least
4tau heavier than its assigned physical item; a surrogate unit row
has zero fluid support. The source-preserving two-endpoint LP rounding
from oct5_arbitrary_heavy_mixed_tiny_physical_palette_audit.txt fixes
zero variables and drops only vertex-color rows with at most four
fractional incident variables, so its additive load increase is at
most 4tau. Every tiny identity receives exactly one physical bulk
color without violating the actual residual capacity.

The two calendar audits agree that the coefficient tends to 1/beta
as epsilon, tau, sigma, and B/Q tend to zero. Rounding M to a multiple
of P costs only O_eta(1) colors. Consequently, for every eta>0 there
is a sufficiently small tau(eta)>0 and a constant C_eta such that

  chi'_w(G) <= (3/2+eta) bp(G) + C_eta

for this restricted matching-heavy/star-forest-tiny physical class.
The period and additive constant are enormous but independent of
graph order and the minimum positive tiny weight. The construction
is finite and polynomial for fixed eta on rational input. It does
not claim 3/2 for a fixed positive tau as bp grows.

RELATED PHYSICAL COMPLETION AND RESIDUAL OVERLAP

Phase 10's direct physical completion lemma remains useful for an
arbitrary feasibly precolored core and arbitrary tiny support. Its
local c^2 K overlap condition, with c=1-4tau, gives an extension in
the same palette, but does not construct a favorable core coloring.
oct5_residual_overlap_full_bound_search.txt states the exact
fixed-core fractional assignment LP and its nonnegative price dual:
for every price vector p on vertex-color rows, the sum over tiny
edges of w_e times the cheapest allowed endpoint-price pair must be
at most the priced residual capacity. The product assignment has an
exact row test that can succeed even when the cruder scalar residual
overlap S_uv>=max(T_u,T_v) fails badly, as in a near-unit heavy
triangle. The note also proves a coefficient-one tiny-only palette
bound ceil(Delta_T/(1-4tau)). A fixed heavy coloring can have poor
residual overlap despite a potentially repairable recoloring; none
of these observations is a lower bound on the unrestricted ratio.

LOWER-BOUND SEARCH AND REMAINING PROBLEMS

oct5_rank_shift_heavy_light_factor_findings.txt records a failed
mixed-medium lower-bound probe. Its bipartite eight-cycle heavy
support and K4,4 smaller-edge pattern have exact two-bin local
packing, but the repeated-pattern pricing oracle ended at numerical
polar value one after 96 cuts. The all-small one-color pattern is
feasible because total small load at each vertex is 0.72. More
generally, if two >1/2 heavy edges already occupy two full local
bins, their combined residual small load is below one, so merely
frustrating which small factor follows which heavy phase cannot
remove the all-small escape in that architecture. This is a search
diagnostic and design constraint, not an exact global upper bound.

The substantial open scope is still unrestricted weighted
multigraphs: nonmatching heavy support, arbitrary branching fluid
support, medium weights, and physical stocks at the half-weight
boundary. The finite calendar eliminates the physical-palette gap
for the restricted mixed star class, but it does not turn the
abstract hybrid law into a theorem for those other topologies.
Future work should seek a common heavy law or exact fixed-core dual
certificate for branching support, and a medium-core decomposition
that preserves the local bin-packing advantage. Do not describe the
restricted 3/2+eta theorem as an improvement to the unchanged
unrestricted [4/3,1.62] and [3/2,1.999] intervals.
