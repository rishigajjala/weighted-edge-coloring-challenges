CONVENTIONS
Each edge has weight in (0,1]. A color is feasible when incident weight at each vertex is at most one. chi'_w is the minimum number of colors. Delta_w is maximum incident weight. bp(G) is the maximum, over vertices, of the minimum number of unit bins needed for the incident edges. Parallel edges have distinct physical identities. In research flow notes, fluid is divisible load; a fluid theorem is not automatically a physical edge-coloring theorem. Physical heavy weights in the new calendar theorem are STRICTLY greater than 1/2.

1. Mixed unequal-star fluid transfer. Read oct5_mixed_star_forest_fixed_hybrid_beta_eps_transfer.txt and oct5_mixed_star_forest_fixed_hybrid_independent_audit.txt. Heavy categorical sources have matching simple support, weights in [1/2,1], and total target at most one per source. Fluid support is a vertex-disjoint star forest, with arbitrary unequal loads, parallel identities, and heavy pairs shared across stars. For 0<epsilon<=1/6, kappa>=64/epsilon and caps C_e>=kappa*y_e, one global hybrid law supports exact (2/3-epsilon)-scaled heavy and fluid means whenever local weighted target rows are feasible. The fixed law is 3/4 independent source occupancy plus 1/4 common-uniform occupancy, initially at heavy marginals alpha*x for alpha=2/3. Conditional fluid means are beta*y, beta=2/3-epsilon, BEFORE final heavy thinning. This resolves the phase-10 unequal-star gluing gap for this class.
Supporting proofs: oct5_endpoint_reduction_universal_mixed_law.txt, oct5_mixed_all_strong_star_fixed_hybrid_audit.txt, and oct5_mixed_one_strong_group_fixed_hybrid_audit.txt. Older unresolved-gap paragraphs in those supporting notes are superseded by the full theorem and phase-11 checkpoint.

2. Bounded source labels. Read oct5_bounded_labels_mixed_star_hybrid_audit.txt. A heavy pair meets at most two stars. Mark large leaf sources and center sources, color their conflicts, and split remaining source classes using two-row assignment rounding. O(kappa^2) labels preserve all prefix arguments, including unguarded leaves. The bound is independent of graph size. Labels alone do not solve denominators; the next construction does.

3. Finite physical transfer. Read oct5_finite_calendar_mixed_star_forest_physical_transfer_audit.txt and oct5_physical_mixed_star_finite_calendar_independent_audit.txt. For every eta>0, there is tau(eta)>0 and C_eta such that chi'_w <= (3/2+eta)bp + C_eta when physical heavy weights exceed 1/2, heavy simple support is a matching, and every other edge has weight at most tau on vertex-disjoint star-forest support. Arbitrary tiny parallel multiplicity and star degree are allowed. A fixed Q-grid period P=4Q^(2L+1), upward surrogate heavy weights, and a dummy unit type give exact bulk heavy counts. Use the PRE-thinning alpha heavy law. Include the dummy in local row checks, then delete dummy slots. Repair leftover heavy stock in matching-only colors. Conditional fluid LPs give exact physical tiny stock, while 4*tau capacity reserve pays for support-preserving iterative rounding. Both calendar audits found no stock/rounding gap conditional on the abstract lemmas. The period is huge but depends only on eta. This does not give factor 3/2 for a fixed positive tau, and does not cover arbitrary medium edges, nonmatching heavy support, or arbitrary tiny branching.

4. General physical completion tool from phase 10. Read oct5_arbitrary_heavy_mixed_tiny_physical_palette_audit.txt and oct5_physical_heavy_tiny_product_rounding_audit.txt. A feasibly precolored arbitrary core can be extended by tiny physical edges in the same K colors under the explicit local condition c^2*K >= max(T_u,T_v)+c*(W_u+W_v), c=1-4*tau. W and T are core and tiny weighted degrees. This sufficient condition uses a product assignment and two-row LP rounding. It does not construct a good core coloring. Read oct5_residual_overlap_full_bound_search.txt for the exact fractional assignment dual, a less wasteful row test, a near-unit triangle example, and the tiny-only coefficient-one palette bound.

More unit-only motifs are not new physical improvements. Concentrate on mixed residuals, medium weights, and genuinely new joint laws.
