# Independent interface audit of the matching-heavy palette bridge

3 October 2026. This checks `sparsify_localize_type_palette_bridge_candidate.md`, not an unrestricted theorem. The proposed scope is bipartite combined support, matching heavy simple support with arbitrary parallel heavy identities, and all remaining physical edges of weight at most a fixed `tau_epsilon`. The asserted palette coefficient has constants independent of graph order, physical multiplicity, and minimum positive tiny weight, subject to the capped paired-activation theorem and the Harris--Srinivasan many-sums proposition.

1. **Forest locality and caps.** For fixed pruned target `tildeY`, decompose it into extreme points `y^k` of the bipartite degree polytope. A term can have cap `12y^k_e`, possibly larger than `12tildeY_e`. The component policy after depth-residue deletion is local to one bounded physical tree and its at most `B` matched-pair IDs. Capped forest activation plus opposite-shore pair correlation supplies every nonnegative-profit support inequality on that component; downward-closed separation supplies one coordinatewise policy independent of the profit vector. Averaging all `k` and residue terms *inside each color* restores cap `12tildeY_e`, and averaging sampled supports restores cap `12y_e/(1+eta)`. A pair ID appearing in two disjoint physical components reuses the same state but does not couple their vertex-capacity constraints.

2. **Collision marking.** If the unpruned sampled pair-graph ball at a root `P` has no repeated final type, no retained component term incident to a port of `P` is discarded. In one sampled support, the sum of termwise edge caps incident to a port is at most `12D'_P<=12` after the convex term average. Thus expected collision loss is at most `12delta_col` per physical port. Marking a bundle when its loss exceeds `theta y_e` gives total normalized marked target at every physical vertex at most `12delta_col/theta`. This is an aggregate statement; individual rare bundles may lose all coverage. The separate all-tiny palette costs `O(delta_col M/theta)+1` colors independently of minimum tiny weight.

3. **Exact heavy stock.** With `M` a multiple of `L=q^d`, bucket `b` at matched pair `P` receives `m_(P,b)=floor(q n_(P,b)/M)` field symbols and exactly `M m_(P,b)/q` main slots. Assign distinct physical identities to these slots. Each bucket leaves fewer than `M/q` identities, so at most `K_b M/q` heavy-only extra colors suffice globally because heavy simple support is a matching. For any bounded local term whose distinct pair IDs have distinct types, the corresponding Vandermonde forms give exactly independent uniform field symbols over each block, irrespective of fixed shifts; hence its block average equals its product categorical expectation. The field size may be selected from the *uniform bound* on the number of types before `M` and the actual labeling are chosen, avoiding circular dependence on `y(M)`.

4. **Whole-edge rounding.** After multiplying normalized tiny flow by `s=1-3tau` and splitting a bundle proportionally among its physical edges, the final averaged per-edge cap is at most its physical weight for sufficiently large `M`, independent of that weight's minimum. Scaling each unmarked edge's fractions down gives source equation `sum_c x_(e,c)=1`. Every option touches exactly its two endpoint/color load rows with amount `w_e<=tau`; the manuscript's two-row option lemma therefore raises each fractional row load by at most `2tau`. Upward heavy buckets with pseudo weight `min(1,bar w/s)` leave at least `3tau` slack in every row with a positive tiny option. When the pseudo weight is one, there is no tiny option at that endpoint, so rounding cannot add one there. This assigns every unmarked physical tiny edge exactly once and preserves every heavy identity assignment.

The proof still depends on the separately audited capped paired-activation and HS inputs. It does not address intermediate weights, interacting heavy bundles, or nonbipartite combined support. No manuscript or repository file was changed.
