PROVED, SCOPED FLUID STATEMENT
Full proof and independent audit:
tmp/weighted-proof/tight-bound-search-2026-10-03/unit_guard_capped_star_hybrid_two_thirds_audited.md

Assumptions: all heavy edges have weight exactly 1; their simple support is a matching, with categorical parallel physical identities allowed. Divisible fluid edges form a vertex-disjoint star forest, not necessarily bipartite together with the heavy edges. The target (x,y) obeys local capacity and categorical constraints. Each fluid edge has one-color cap C_e >= 12 y_e. Conclusion: (2/3)x for every heavy identity and (2/3)y for every fluid edge have a common one-color convex-hull realization. This is a fractional fluid-hull result, not an integer stock coloring or a theorem for arbitrary tiny-edge support.

One fixed, profit-independent heavy law: with probability 3/4 sample each heavy pair independently with categorical marginals (2/3)x; with probability 1/4 use one common uniform U, making pair P free iff U <= 1-(2/3)x_P and choosing an occupied identity conditionally from x_h/x_P. Both modes individually give every heavy identity marginal (2/3)x_h. For each profit prefix in a star, center target s and leaf-pair groups of target Y_i are reduced pessimistically to d_i=Y_i/2, activation (1+2d_i)/3, and aggregate cap c_i=min(1,24d_i). The proof divides groups at d_i=1/24, bounding independent failure factors by (2/3)(1-d_i) for strong groups and exp(-8d_i) for weak groups. The comonotone mode pays the strong-group or all-weak deficit. It also handles a center paired directly to one of its own leaves. Greedy star recourse simultaneously maximizes every decreasing-profit prefix, so the prefix bound integrates to arbitrary nonnegative q; support separation gives exact fluid marginals under the same heavy law. Shared heavy sources across different stars do not affect expected additive star recourse.

SIMPLE UNIFORM-d SUFFICIENT REGIME (PROVED DIRECTLY)
Suppose every fluid-target vertex has total target load at most a common d in [0,1], and every guarded vertex has heavy residual allowance at least d. Under the same fixed hybrid law, when d >= 1/12 route y_e/d on each fluid edge whose endpoint heavy sources are free; when d <= 1/12 route 12 y_e there. Every state is feasible because incident routed load is at most 1, and C_e >= 12y_e. For distinct heavy pairs, endpoint availability is at least B(d)=(3/4)p^2+(1/4)p with p=(1+2d)/3; same-pair or unguarded endpoints only improve availability. The identity B(d)-(2/3)d=(1-d+2d^2)/6>0 proves the d >= 1/12 case. For d <= 1/12, B(d)>=1/6 gives routed marginal at least 2y_e. This works for arbitrary fluid support but requires the global common load/residual parameter; it does not imply the unrestricted theorem.

MAIN GAPS
1. The fixed comonotone hybrid is unproved for arbitrary nonstar fluid support, especially heterogeneous residual profiles and odd fluid cores. The diagnostics above are only small cases.
2. The audited star proof uses binary residuals of unit heavy guards. Heterogeneous heavy weights yield real residual profiles, and common-quantile ordering by mean is invalid without an additional argument.
3. No new unrestricted multigraph integer coloring bound follows. Exact physical tiny-edge stocks, capped locality beyond this star scope, and heavy supports beyond a matching remain separate tasks.
